Keep one legal route

Runners take the shortest route, including diagonals. They may round one object diagonally, but cannot squeeze through when both side tiles are occupied. Demolishing an eligible generated rock or slow tower costs 8 gold; illegal actions cost nothing.

  • Tower of Lament: any of the eight adjacent tiles triggers 50% speed for 5 seconds, followed by its 5-second recharge.
  • Neutral speed tower: uses the same 0/1/2 spawn rates as slow towers. Any of the eight adjacent tiles grants +50% speed for 5 seconds. It cannot be built or removed.
  • Linked portal: has a 25% chance to place two linked ends at least three squares apart. The runner never seeks one, but entering an end moves it to its partner before rerouting normally. Each pair deactivates independently after one use.
  • Trap Door: uses the slow-tower spawn rates. The runner never seeks one, but crossing it launches up to three squares forward, stopping at the last valid edge square, then deactivates.
  • Endless Feast: appears in 20% of floors. Every runner must reach the protected checkpoint before heading to the portal and has Insatiable Hunger on the first leg. You can block its surrounding squares as long as one cardinal side remains open.

Stop the escape

Outwit. Outbuild. Outlast.

Descend through five increasingly large fields. Spend gold and rare Tears of the Runner to create the longest legal route, then release the runners and see whose maze wastes the most time. Runners can round one object diagonally, but cannot squeeze between two diagonally touching objects.

Each seed chooses a rectangle, diamond, donut, or flower-petal field. Dark cells outside its silhouette are unplayable. Generated rocks and neutral slow towers can be demolished for 8 gold; speed towers, linked portals, and trap doors are fixed to the field.

In 20% of floors, Endless Feast becomes a mandatory checkpoint before the portal.

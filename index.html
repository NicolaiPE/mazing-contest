Keep one legal route

Runners take the shortest route, including diagonals. They may round one object diagonally, but cannot squeeze through when both side tiles are occupied. Demolishing a generated object costs 8 gold; illegal actions cost nothing.

  • Tower of Lament: an orthogonally adjacent tile triggers 50% speed for 5 seconds, followed by its 5-second recharge.
  • Neutral speed tower: one appears in 25% of floors and an orthogonally adjacent tile grants +100% speed for 5 seconds. It is part of the shared map and cannot be built.
  • Endless Feast: appears in 20% of floors. Every runner must reach the protected checkpoint before heading to the portal and has Insatiable Hunger on the first leg. You can block its surrounding squares as long as one cardinal side remains open.

The guild gates are open

Outwit. Outbuild. Outlast.

Descend through four increasingly large fields. Spend gold and rare Tears of the Runner to create the longest legal route, then release the runners and see whose maze wastes the most time. Runners can round one object diagonally, but cannot squeeze between two diagonally touching objects.

Each seed chooses a rectangle, diamond, donut, or flower-petal field. Dark cells outside its silhouette are unplayable. Any generated rock or neutral tower can be demolished for 8 gold.

In 20% of floors, Endless Feast becomes a mandatory checkpoint before the portal.
